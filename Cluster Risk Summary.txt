Savvy Nomad (CRITICAL LOSS of greatest $$$)
- Top tier consumers that hate long contracts, and have 0 tolerance;
- If a One-Year bundle gets sneaked up, survivability flattens;
- Two-Year might be a stretch, but with right bundles (and trust) it's possible;
- Compell with top-notch quality and irresistible deals;
- Act immediately, month 0 to 3 onboarding.

Traditionalist (HIGH LOSS of customers and revenue + RLV)
- Leans to One-Year and DSL, and utilities;
- Focus on Value-Added Services (non-essentials, security, streaming);
- Work towards Two-Year + VAS for maximum retention;
- Campaign on month 6 (slip contract change) and 7-12.

Niche Surfer (MODERATE LOSS, and smallest group)
- Internet only, no phone; likes good net services;
- Aside from bundling, prove service quality improvement;
- Act on months 12 and 13-18, try a One-Year to Two-Year lock.

Stable Bundler (LOW-MODERATE LOSS, greatest cust. count at risk)
- Mainly landline user, quite tolerant;
- Due to long survival, no reason to waste many bundles;
- Auto messaging with slight discounts for longer contract will do (initially); 
- Act from 18th month onward, and try Two-Year by month 18.

Loyalist (NO RISK, for now)
- Loves VAS content with the telco ecosystem;
- Act with cross-selling for assuring survival and satisfaction;
- Plan campaigns with partner releases and trends;
- Locking in Two-Year should be easy.

States
- Looking at State data, Internet_Type and Payment_Method create friction in different degrees;
- Where service operations are churn drivers, audits should be ran prior to bundling/deals. 
- Fix operations in critical regions, then lock in longer contracts with quality services
- Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka, and Maharashtra have countless Savvies; critical implementation of bundles 
- Uttar Pradesh has a lot of Stable Bundlers; deal longer contracts and improve services to address

! None of this accounts for Uplift analysis yet.
- Only post-implementation, in a tailored Test/Control setting, may the behaviors be inferred.
- Stable Bundlers and Tradionalists could surprise and hide many Sleeping Dogs; who knows...
- Regardless, acting is obligatory for all states and most clusters, especially minding RLV
- User activity, feedback and NPS, CAC and informed churn reasons must also be used to guide campaigns.
